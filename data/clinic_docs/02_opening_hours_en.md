# Opening hours

## Regular hours

Consultations and the reception desk are open Saturday to Thursday, from 08:00 to 18:00. The clinic is closed on Friday, except for the emergency unit.

## Emergency unit

The emergency unit is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Fridays and public holidays. Use entrance B on Rue Ibn Sina.

## Laboratory

Blood and urine samples are taken Saturday to Thursday from 07:30 to 11:00, without an appointment. Results can be collected at the laboratory desk from 13:00 to 17:00.

## Imaging (radiology and ultrasound)

Saturday to Thursday, from 08:30 to 16:00, by appointment only.

## Ramadan hours

During the month of Ramadan, consultations run Saturday to Thursday from 09:00 to 15:00. The laboratory takes samples from 08:30 to 11:00. The emergency unit stays open 24/7.

## Public holidays

On national and religious holidays (for example Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, 1 November, 5 July) only the emergency unit is open.
