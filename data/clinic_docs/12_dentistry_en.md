# Dentistry department

The dentist, Dr Amine Khelifi, sees patients Saturday to Thursday from 09:00 to 17:00, by appointment.

## Services and prices

- Dental check-up: 2000 DZD.
- Scaling and cleaning: 3000 DZD.
- Tooth extraction: 2500 DZD.
- Filling (composite): 4000 DZD.
- Root canal treatment: from 8000 DZD, depending on the tooth.
- Crowns and dentures: price given after a check-up.

## Dental emergencies

For strong tooth pain during opening hours, call to get a same-day slot if one is available. At night and on Fridays, the general emergency unit can see you, but there is no dentist on duty.

## Insurance

Dental care is paid in full at the clinic. You receive a care sheet (feuille de soins) to claim reimbursement from CNAS or CASNOS.

## Children

Children from 4 years old can be seen by the dentist, with a parent present.
