# Laboratory

The laboratory takes samples Saturday to Thursday from 07:30 to 11:00. No appointment is needed. Please bring your prescription and your Chifa card.

## Fasting tests

For fasting blood sugar (glycemia), cholesterol and lipid tests, do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before the sample. Drinking plain water is allowed. Come in the morning.

## Tests without fasting

A complete blood count (FNS) does not require fasting unless your doctor said otherwise.

## Urine test

Collect the first urine of the morning in a sterile container. Containers are available free of charge at the laboratory desk.

## Medication

Keep taking your usual medication unless your doctor told you otherwise. Tell the laboratory staff which medication you take.

## Results

Most results are ready within 24 hours. Some special tests take 48 to 72 hours. You will receive an SMS when the results are ready. Results are collected at the laboratory desk from 13:00 to 17:00 and are given only to the patient or a parent for a child. Results are not given over the phone.
