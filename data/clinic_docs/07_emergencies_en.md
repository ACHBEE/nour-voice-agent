# Emergency unit

The emergency unit of Clinique El Amal is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including Fridays and holidays. No appointment is needed. The entrance is entrance B, on Rue Ibn Sina, at the back of the building.

## Life-threatening situations

If someone has chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, signs of a stroke, or a serious accident, call 14 (Civil Protection) immediately or come straight to the emergency unit. Do not wait for a callback or an appointment.

## What the phone assistant can and cannot do

The voice assistant Nour cannot assess symptoms or give medical advice. For any urgent health problem, it will tell you to come to the emergency unit or to call 14.

## Children

Children are seen in the emergency unit at any hour. A pediatrician can be called in during the day.

## Price

An emergency consultation costs 2500 DZD from 08:00 to 20:00 and 3000 DZD at night. Payment can be made after care.
