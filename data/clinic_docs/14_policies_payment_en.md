# Clinic policies

## Visiting hours

The clinic has a small day-stay observation unit. Visits are allowed every day from 13:00 to 15:00 and from 18:00 to 19:30, one visitor per patient at a time. Children under 12 are not allowed in the observation unit.

## Privacy

Medical information is confidential. Results and medical records are given only to the patient, or to a parent or legal guardian for a minor. No medical information is shared over the phone.

## Call logging

Calls answered by the voice assistant Nour may be logged to improve the service. Bookings and callback requests are stored securely in the clinic system.

## Payment

Payment is made at the reception desk in cash or with a CIB or Edahabia card. Cheques are not accepted. An invoice is given on request.

## Complaints and feedback

Complaints can be left at the reception desk or by asking Nour for a callback from the patient relations office.

## Smoking

Smoking is forbidden inside the clinic and in the parking area.
